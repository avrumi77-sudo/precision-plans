## About Us

Tri-State As-Built Pros delivers accurate, code-compliant as-built surveys and drawings across the New York, New Jersey, and Connecticut tri-state region. Whether you're closing out a permit, verifying construction against approved plans, or documenting an existing structure for a sale or renovation, our licensed surveyors and drafters get it right the first time — no back-and-forth with the building department, no costly delays.

We work directly with contractors, architects, engineers, real estate attorneys, and homeowners, turning field measurements into clean, stamped, submission-ready drawings.

## Services

### As-Built Surveys
Field-verified surveys documenting the exact as-constructed condition of a property — structures, additions, decks, pools, and site improvements — compared against approved plans.

### As-Built Drawings & CAD Drafting
Precise, submission-ready CAD drawings converted from field data, redlines, or hand sketches. Formatted to meet local building department and DOB requirements.

### Elevation Certificates
FEMA-compliant elevation certificates for flood zone compliance, insurance, and permit close-out.

### Foundation Location Surveys
Fast turnaround foundation surveys required before framing can proceed.

### Topographic Surveys
Detailed site topography for design, drainage, and grading plans.

### ALTA/NSPS Land Title Surveys
Comprehensive boundary and title surveys for commercial real estate transactions and financing.

### Site Plans & Permit Support
Site plans prepared for zoning applications, variances, and permit submissions — plus support responding to building department comments.
